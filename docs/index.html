How to Play

Objective

Reduce your opponent's 3 lives to 0.

Turn Structure

  1. Mana Phase — Gain 1 max mana (max 5), refill to max. Start with 1 mana.
  2. Main Phase — Summon creatures, play Cast Verses, set Set Verses.
  3. Battle Phase — Attack with your active creature.
  4. End Phase — Pass turn to opponent.

Cards

Creatures — Summon to the field to fight. Each has HP, ATK, and a unique ability. One active creature fights; up to 2 on bench as backup.

Cast Verses — Immediate effects. Pay mana, resolve effect, goes to graveyard.

Set Verses — Placed face-down. Trigger automatically when conditions are met.

Combat

Your active creature attacks the enemy's active creature. Damage reduces HP. When HP hits 0, creature is KO'd and goes to graveyard.

Direct Damage: If opponent has no active creature, your attack takes 1 of their lives!

Winning

  • Take all 3 of your opponent's lives
  • Enemy can't draw (deck out)

Controls

Desktop: [S] Summon, [C] Cast, [T] Set Verse, [A] Attack, [R] Retreat, [E] End Turn

Mobile: Tap buttons. Long-press any card to see details.
